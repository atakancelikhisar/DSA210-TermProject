# AI Usage Documentation (DSA 210)

Following the project guidelines, here are sample prompts used during the development of this project:

1. **Prompt:** "How to fix the convergence warning in Logistic Regression for a small dataset in scikit-learn?"
   **Output:** Suggestion to increase the `max_iter` parameter or scale features using `StandardScaler`.
2. **Prompt:** "Help me interpret a p-value of 0.59 in the context of a Point-Biserial correlation for game data."
   **Output:** Explanation that the correlation is not statistically significant and the null hypothesis cannot be rejected.
3. **Prompt:** "In my Clash Royale dataset, the 'average_elixir_cost' feature shows very low variance. How should I discuss this limitation in my report, and does it affect the coefficients of my Logistic Regression model?"
   **Output:** Advice on acknowledging feature limitations in the 'Limitations' section and an explanation of how low variance can lead to unstable coefficient estimates.
